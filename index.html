01 — About

Engineer's mindset, designer's eye.

I'm Mithun Rahman, a full-stack developer and UI/UX designer who builds production-grade web applications end to end. My core strength is PHP & Laravel backend architecture — designing normalized database schemas, domain-driven service layers, secure authentication, and RESTful APIs that stay fast under load.
